Amino Acids:
  -Over 300 amino acids
  -20(23) are used to build proteins
    -Capacity to polymerize
    -Useful acid-base properties
    -Varied properties and functions
  -α-carbon
    -Always 4 substituents 
      -All (except proline) are connected to the carbon
    -Acidic Carboxyl group
    -Basic Amino group
    -Hydrogen
    -Unique R group
  -Zwitterion = Amino acid with protonated Amino group (NH3+) and deprotonated carboxyl group (COO-)
  -Amino acids can act as buffers in 2 ranges 
  -Proteins only contain L-amino acids
    -L-molecules rotate polarized light to the left
    -D-molecules rotate polarized light to the right
  -All amino acids (except glycine) are chiral

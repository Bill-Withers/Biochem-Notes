Suffixes:
  -ose = sugar
  -ase = enzyme

Kinase: Adds phosphate
Phosphatase: Removes phosphate

Living Matter:
  -High degree of chemical complexity & organization
  -Extraction, transformation, and use of energy
  -Components with defined functions with regulated interactions 
  -Sense and respond to stimuli
  -Self replication and assembly, gradual evolution

Cell Types:
  -Bacterial
  -Animal
  -Plant

Organisms are never at equilibrium with their environment 

Living Systems extract energy:
  -Constant energy input necessary for life
  -Sunlight:
      -Plants
      -Cyanobacteria
      -Green Bacteria
  -Fuels:
    -Animals
    -Most bacteria

DNA = Linear polymer of nucleotides
A-T bond = 2 Hydrogen bonds
C-G bond = 3 Hydrogen bonds

Replication = DNA Strand is separated, a new strand is made to complement the strands
Mutation = Broken pair replaced with a different pair

Methods of Evolution:
  -Endosymbiosis: Eukaryote absorbs bacteria/prokaryote and gains functions. Bacterial genes enter nucleus and divide with Eukaryote.
  -Random Mutation/Gene duplication:
    -Natural selection favors some mutations 

Origin of Life:
  -Hydrogen, Nitrogen, Carbon, and Oxygen make up 99% mass of most cells
  -Mass of human body:
    -Hydrogen(10%)
    -Nitrogen(3%)
    -Carbon(18%)
    -Oxygen(65%)
  -Miller-Urey Experiment:
    -Synthesis of basic biomolecules from primitive atmospheric conditions with spark
    -Possible explanation for origin of life

RNA is an info carrier and biocatalyst

Common Cell Features:
  -Cytoplasm = Everything in cell
  -Cytosol = Solution of enzymes, RNA, metabolites, coenzymes, ribosomes, etc.
  -Nucleus = genome storage and replication 

Bacterial cell features:
  (See Bacterial Cell picture)
  -Some bacteria retain Gram Stain (Blue)
  -Gram positive = single membrane of peptidoglycan (porous)
  -Gram negative = Double membrane with outer membrane made of lipopolysaccharides and phospholipids on the inside

Eukaryotes:
  -Membrane enclosed organelles
  -Red blood cells have no nucleus
  -Nucleus does selective import/export
  -Energy production in mitochondria/chloroplast
